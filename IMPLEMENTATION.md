# Worldwide world contract — v0.7

The user's October 23 revision supersedes the regional round/budget parts of GAME_PLAN.md.

- Full viewport worldwide flat map. Geographic bounds -180..180 longitude, -90..90 latitude; grid step 2 degrees (180 columns × 90 rows), south-to-north rows, west-to-east columns, cell centres.
- Startup camera: immediate Southeast Asia regional fallback at 115°E/10°N (60° span across the shorter viewport dimension), then browser geolocation to a 36° regional span when available. Clamp to camera limits, use an 8 s deadline, and ignore late results after interaction/disposal. No external location service or location persistence. Map reset returns to the configured home; home framing adapts to resize and full-world zoom-out remains available.
- Live continuous simulation: the world starts empty; natural formation and past typhoons are switched on from the sidebar. No budget, costs, round deadline or game-over screen. Pause/speed/reset remain explicit controls. Worker init/reset starts playing at 1×; replay loads a paused snapshot.
- Warm, moisture, low and high tools are free. Bottom icons support pointer drag/drop on the map and accessible click/keyboard placement. Overlapping warm/moist/low conditions at suitable ocean latitude accumulate time before generating a depression. Each placed low is an independent candidate, including nearby or overlapping lows. Completing genesis consumes only that low; it never absorbs neighbouring candidates or resets their progress. A depression gradually strengthens and its visible circulation grows. Southern circulation is reversed; longitude distances/movement wrap at the dateline.
- `GameState.storms` replaces the old singular storm; each storm owns numeric id, full track, forecast and `history: StormSample[]`. `stormArchive: StormRecord[]` retains ended systems with outcome, ending hour and merger target when relevant. `candidates` records forming patches. No old `resources`, `endReason`, global track/forecast or finished phase. See src/types.ts.
- Ten tools in one palette, in `TOOLS` order (`warm`, `moisture`, `low`, `high`, `warm-air`, `updraft`, `cool`, `dry`, `cold-air`, `downdraft`). Keys 1–9 select the first nine and 0 the tenth; the 10 columns above 48 rem become 5 × 2 below it. Coordinate placement offers every tool. Scalar effects use the same ramp, fade and spatial falloff. Warm air adds 3 °C per full effect (signed sum capped at ±6); updraft adds 1 m/s (signed sum capped at ±3). Air temperature is base thermal field −1.5 °C + capped air heating, independent of ocean-heating tools. The cached grid and point sampler calculate identical added fields.
- Cool water subtracts 3 °C per full effect with a 9 °C total cooling cap and ocean-only placement. Dry air subtracts 0.3 humidity per full effect before the shared [0,1] clamp. Cold air contributes −3 °C and downdraft −1 m/s to the shared signed air fields. Sampling and grid calculations apply the same falloff, fade, ramp and caps.
- Convective support = 1 + 0.04 × airHeating + 0.12 × updraft (0.4–1.6). It multiplies genesis potential and positive ocean strengthening. Negative air fields additionally scale the intensity target by clamp(1 − 0.1 × cold − 0.15 × sinking, 0, 1) and add decay of 0.2 × cold + 0.4 × sinking + 1.5 × drying m/s per hour. Existing moisture/shear/Coriolis gates and low-pressure seeding remain intact. This is a tuned game rule, not a resolved convection model. Without the new tools the multiplier is exactly 1, preserving existing four-tool trajectories. In v0.7, longer natural decay changes trajectories; legacy sandbox imports are recalculated under current rules.
- `GameState.genesis` (`enabled`, `waves`, `nextSpawnTick`) drives natural formation; `GameState.historic` lists loaded past typhoons and `nextHistoricStormId` allocates their IDs. Automatic lows get IDs from 50000 and historic storms from 100000. Automatic waves choose from five tropical western-Pacific launch areas with seeded ocean-only jitter and place warm water, moisture, low pressure, warm air and updraft, all aged zero with normal tool durations. Automatic effect IDs are negative and unique per wave; player actions remain positive. Genesis switches and historic loads enter the replay log as actions; automatic waves do not. Normal candidate progress, genesis and intensity rules apply: no storm is injected directly. New waves are scheduled 48–72 h later; if active storms plus pending lows reach three, or five new effects would exceed the 64-effect limit, postpone four hours. Forecast clones never trigger automatic waves.
- UI reset obtains a fresh crypto seed so natural formation differs per game; raw worker resets keep the current seed when omitted. Replay is gated by the expected seed, tick and an action signature covering placements, genesis switches and historic loads. The Show recorded paths switch is UI-only state (`localStorage`), passed to the overlay painter, which draws each loaded storm's full best track dashed and wrapped across the dateline. Damage metrics use current cumulative city estimates, not a sum of news bulletins. Average lifetime includes dissipated records only and measures ending hour minus first history sample hour.
- Storm motion uses shared, bounded mutual steering for live movement, wind asymmetry and forecasts: same-hemisphere rotation, close-range convergence, smooth 1,000 km cutoff, summed perturbation capped at 8 m/s, maturity ramp over 24 h. Stable ID summation and simultaneous pre-move snapshots avoid array-order dependence. No self-vortex steering. Forecasts step all cloned centres together at 0.25 h, with intensity/tools frozen and merger rules shared.
- `Storm.movementSpeedKmh` stores actual last-step centre displacement divided by model hours, initially 0; merger repositioning is excluded. Both languages show it separately from wind in the storm list and detail view.
- Mature storms (age ≥24 h, wind ≥17.2 m/s) in the same hemisphere merge within 100 km. Closest disjoint pairs merge per step; stronger wind wins, with ID tie breaks. Preserve the survivor ID/track, recalculate the centre and pressure/forecast, emit a merger event, and follow selection through mergers. Formation candidates remain independent.
- History samples record hourly metrics, exact stage changes in both directions, birth and ending. Archive dissipated/high-latitude/absorbed systems once; forecast-only merges cannot mutate the archive. Replay regenerates all records; reset clears the current-world archive. Selected archive records draw full routes and grade/time markers, with a dateline-aware camera fit and sidebar-aware label placement.
- Rows/details show outer circulation radius and gale radius; remove exact-centre wind. Map-following labels show the storm ID, current `stageRank` (0–5), outer circulation radius and movement speed in km/h. The grade legend explicitly describes game stages.
- City damage bulletins cross cumulative score/population tiers (0.0005, 0.01, 0.05, 0.15), emitting only the highest crossed tier per city per step. `damage-news.ts` snapshots fictional counts from damage bounded by exposed city population: displaced = floor(0.35 × damage), missing = floor(0.00004 × damage), deaths = floor(0.00001 × damage), flooded homes = floor(displaced × 0.65 / 3), losses = damage × USD 12,500 rounded to USD 1,000. `estimateStormDamage` applies the same rates to a storm's own `damageScore`. Generic residential areas are stable per city; no flood geography is computed. News figures are cumulative snapshots, not additional losses, and never feed back into physics. News remains derived presentation; it never changes the underlying damage score.
- Storms may dissipate individually, while the world continues. State phase is seeding when no storms and active otherwise. The sidebar Ranking orders storms by estimated loss, displaced people, deaths, flooded homes, peak grade (`stageRank(peakWind)`) or survival time (ending or current hour minus first history sample hour), top 10, ties by loss then earlier record; zero values are left out of each metric's ranking. Each ended storm becomes one `RankingEntry` keyed `seed:stormId:hash`, where hash is 32-bit FNV-1a (hex) of the actions up to the storm's end tick (`src/ui/rankings.ts`), so the same seed + actions re-import idempotently (first `recordedAt` kept) and other sessions never collide. Entries are upserted into a `RankingStore` (localStorage `typhoon-lab-rankings`, strictly validated, newest 500); the store is re-read before each write, so tabs only overwrite each other's same-key entries. Active storms are merged for display only. The store interface is the seam for the planned server upload.
- Replay uses seed, actions, and explicit `untilTick`. Actions are placements, `genesis` switches (`on`) and `historic` loads (`typhoonId`). It is deterministic and bounded for validation, independently of the live session's unlimited elapsed time. Engine owns the exported validation constants and functions. Replay files are format version 4 / simulation 0.8.0 and carry no mode or typhoon id; other versions are rejected clearly. A loaded typhoon replays a recorded JMA best track by time interpolation (`trackAt` in the engine) instead of moving and intensifying the storm, so the worker registers each needed reanalysis before replaying.
- Map fills the viewport; only the right sidebar consumes panel space. Branding, layers, clock/play controls, legend and bottom weather palette float over the map. Right panel collapses on mobile. No left sidebar or stacked page scrolling.
- Freeze simulation time and field/particle/overlay/traffic animation during pan, pinch and wheel gestures; still redraw the camera so dragging moves the map. Resume from the same checkpoint without accumulating elapsed gesture time, preserving the user's play/pause and speed settings. Expose data-interacting on map-stage for integration checks.
- Cap the fleet at 10 aircraft and 16 ships, painting aircraft above ships. Scale active traffic to the number of unique visible airport/port origins, one vehicle per hub and service. Put excess safe vehicles into inactive reserve; they neither move nor render. Reuse reserve/offscreen vehicles for staggered departures from visible hubs, preserving retained visible journeys. Never revive accidents or interrupt diversions/exposure. Record dispatches and reserve changes separately from weather actions and include them in replay reconstruction and ranking signatures.
- Keep synthetic weather/population clearly labelled. Coastlines and city coordinates are real. Global Natural Earth bundle generated reproducibly with pinned source checksums.

## October 23 v0.7 additions

- One combined map replaces exclusive display modes. `MapOverlays` independently toggles wind particles, humidity colours, pressure contours, SST tint and population tint/markers, all enabled initially. Particles sample local relative humidity alongside velocity; both renderers use the same 16-step dark-blue-to-pale-white ramp with increasing luminance. Humidity off restores wind colours; humidity on never enables hidden wind automatically. Wind off skips particle motion/drawing in both renderers. `buildCombinedImage` composites population over ocean-only SST (alpha 64/255); wind and pressure have no competing heatmaps. Both base renderers consume the same image. Isobars use 8 hPa spacing from 1000 hPa up and 20 hPa spacing below; levels at or below 1000 hPa are thicker. Shared-edge contour tracing preserves whole paths across the date line, then two Chaikin passes soften grid corners. Isobars retain dark casing for contrast. Overlay buttons use `aria-pressed`, and the legend lists enabled overlays.
- Natural formation and recorded paths use accessible `role="switch"` buttons (On/Off, 켜짐/꺼짐); the past-typhoon select only loads on the Load typhoon button. Condition buttons retain the canonical full names at every viewport width.
- 71 cities are generated from the pinned Natural Earth source, with fictional demonstration populations. Added Korea/Japan/China/Philippines coverage and cyclone coasts worldwide.
- Natural ocean intensity relaxation is 96 h, reduced toward 20 h by weakening tools. Natural shear loss scales with the relaxation time; inland decay is softened from 0.095 to 0.075 per hour. This is not a fixed lifetime.
- `GameState.thunderstorms` stores deterministic short-lived convective cells: positions, intensity, gusts, radius, approximate cloud tops, age and duration. Warm/moist/rising air triggers them over land or sea; dry/cold/sinking air suppresses them. Their localized gust damage shares the capped maximum-damage accounting and city news with cyclones. Untouched sandbox climate stays calm.
- Weakening condition Korean labels: Cool water 차가운 바다, Dry air 건조한 공기, Cold air 차가운 공기, Downdraft 하강 기류, High pressure 고기압.
